# Launch-readiness audit — pops.bg, 2026-10-31

**Question answered:** are we ready to accept users (free members) and customers (paying clubs)?
**Audited build:** `origin/main` df71fdc (PR #49 on launch PR #47), live at https://pops.bg since 2026-10-31 20:11 UTC.
**Method:** three read-only code explorations, the full test and CI chain on a production build, a 400-page anonymous crawl and Lighthouse/perf traces of the live site, a gstack design review of 14 live pages (desktop + 390 px), a visual review of 25 signed-in screens (desktop + mobile), persona journeys on a local server with the production code, two adversarial code reviews (payments money path; authz/erasure/export/tokens), a gstack static security run, and a pre-triage against two earlier audits (2026-09-29, 2026-10-10) and the clubs production audit of 2026-10-30. Blockers were fixed as they were confirmed (five PRs, below).
**Ledger:** every finding with its evidence lives in `evidence/2026-10-31/findings-ledger.md`; this document ranks and decides.

## 1. Verdict

### Free members (map, games, passport, contributions): **GO with conditions**

The product is ready: every member journey passed on the production code, the live site serves 400 pages without an error, all suites are green on a production build, and the one member-path S0 (forms lost on every deploy, A-01) is fixed in #50. The conditions are about the organisation around the product, not the product; each has an owner and a date.

### `CLUB_PAYMENTS_ENABLED`: **stays OFF** (separate gate)

Switch it on only after: the payments-gates PR is merged (deauthorize before purge, dashboard-revoke handling, failed-renewal handling, livemode and currency checks); the Stripe Connect application, webhook endpoint and three secrets exist in test mode; the Cloudflare WAF skip rule covers the webhook and API paths; and one test-mode onboarding + checkout + refund round-trip on pops.bg is evidenced. None of this blocks contracts with payments off.

Severity scale: **S0** legal impossibility, data loss, money wrong, or a core journey that cannot complete · **S1** member- or club-visible failure in a core journey, or legal exposure with a cheap fix · **S2** degraded but recoverable · **S3** polish. GO = no open S0 and every S1 fixed or in an approved PR within 7 days; GO with conditions = no open S0, S1s with a named owner and a date within 14 days, listed verbatim; NO-GO = any open S0.
